About

Zero-Rated Hosts TZ ni dashboard ya umma inayoonyesha orodha ya domain zinazojulikana (au zilizoripotiwa) kama zero-rated kwenye mitandao ya simu ya Tanzania (Vodacom, Airtel, Yas, Halotel).

Zero-rating inamaanisha trafiki kwenda host hiyo haikati data yako (au inakatiwa kidogo sana) kulingana na mpango wa operator.

Muhimu – Disclaimer

Detection is approximate only. Mobile operators use Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) and SNI matching. Results shown here are not guaranteed. Zero-rating can depend on your specific plan, time of day, or promotional period. Use this tool at your own risk. This project does not provide free data and is not affiliated with any mobile network operator.

Unaweza ku-report host mpya kupitia ukurasa wa Report Host. Reports zitaonekana kama “Reported” hadi zithibitishwe.

Android scanner app (independent) inatumia TrafficStats API kujaribu kugundua zero-rating kwenye device yako. Hakuna sync kati ya webapp na Android app.
